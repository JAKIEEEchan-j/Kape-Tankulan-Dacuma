How to Order

Welcome to our ordering page! Here's how you can place an order:

  1. Browse our menu and select the items you'd like to order.
  2. Add items to your cart.
  3. Review your order and proceed to checkout.
  4. Enter your delivery information and payment details.
  5. Confirm your order and wait for your delicious coffee!
